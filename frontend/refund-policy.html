Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains how registration fee refunds and cancellations are handled at Everest Aviation Academy.

Registration Fee

The amount paid online while reserving a seat is a registration fee, adjusted against your total course fee once you formally enrol.

Cancellation Before Course Start

  • If you cancel your admission more than 7 days before the course start date, the registration fee (minus a processing charge) may be refunded.
  • If you cancel within 7 days of the course start date, the registration fee is non-refundable.

Cancellation After Course Start

Once training has commenced, the registration fee and any course fee paid are non-refundable, except in cases handled at the sole discretion of academy management.

Refund Process

Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method within 7–14 working days via Razorpay.

Contact for Refunds

To request a refund or cancellation, email info@everestaviationacademy.com or call +91 90330 21835 with your payment reference ID.
